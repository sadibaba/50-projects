# Product Notes

VoltNest is a small, curated electronics store rather than an endless
marketplace. The product decisions are:

1. A short catalogue is easier to trust than a wall of listings.
2. Prices, stock and specifications should be visible before commitment.
3. Checkout should be fast and should never surprise the customer.
4. The owner should be able to run the store without a second admin tool.
5. Mobile is the primary buying surface.

## Customer journey

1. Land on the editorial home page.
2. Browse a category or search the catalogue.
3. Read specs and reviews on a product page.
4. Add to cart and apply a coupon.
5. Sign in at checkout, confirm the address and place the order.
6. Track the order from the account page.

## Owner journey

1. Sign in as an admin.
2. Check revenue, order volume and low-stock products.
3. Add or edit a product.
4. Move orders through packing and shipping.
5. Export orders for accounting.

## What makes it different

- A design language based on paper and ink rather than generic marketplace blue.
- Transparent demo payment language instead of a fake card form.
- Stock verification and coupon rules enforced by the server.
- A compact admin dashboard with low-stock and top-seller signals.
- Free delivery threshold and bundle coupon for larger setups.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Real payment processing.
- Multi-vendor marketplace.
- Product variants and sizing.
- Shipping-provider integrations.
- Returns and refunds workflow.
- Email marketing.

These are listed in the README roadmap instead of being presented as complete.
